  <div class="card">
    <div class="card-body">
      <h4 class="card-title">FIA Database for Ecologists</h4>
      <p class="card-text">
        Led a workshop at the 2023 ESA annual meeting titled <em>"An introduction to estimating ecological change with the USDA Forest Service FIA database"</em>, organized with USFS scientists Harold Zald and Jane Terzibashian, and University of Maine PhD student Bailey McLaughlin.
      </p>
      <p class="card-text">
        The FIA database is an incredibly rich source of ecological data, but can be complicated for new users. The workshop aimed to demystify the database, provide tools for new users, and help ecologists avoid common pitfalls. All materials available in <a href="https://github.com/daniel-perret/FIA-workshop-ESA2023">this GitHub repository</a>.
      </p>

  <h2 class="section-header">Mentoring & Student Training</h2>

  <div class="card mb-3">
    <div class="card-body">
      <h5 class="card-title">Undergraduate Research Mentoring</h5>
      <p class="card-text">
        I've had numerous opportunities to mentor undergraduate researchers in the field and lab. I was the primary mentor for Morgan Florsheim's 2020 undergraduate honors thesis at Brown University, titled <em>"Understanding the role of adventive populations in tree species' ability to track climate change"</em>, training her in dendroecological field work, lab techniques, and analysis.
      </p>
      <p class="card-text">
        I've also trained multiple undergraduates in preparing, processing, and cross-dating tree-ring samples for ecological analyses. Other students I trained in GIS software for ecology now use those skills as professional geospatial analysts and field ecologists.
      </p>
