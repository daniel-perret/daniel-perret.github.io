In this study, titled <em>"Reducing fire severity and extent bolsters subalpine forest resilience to global change through key demographic pathways"</em>, we present a framework for bridging from individual tree responses to ecosystem-level reorganization trajectories. <a href="https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/gcb.70052">Check it out here</a>.
